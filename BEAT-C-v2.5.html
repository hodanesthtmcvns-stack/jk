Infection + acute organ dysfunction

Conventional SOFA score (0–24). Enter observed values for the same clinical assessment. Suspected/confirmed infection is taken from the Evidence of infection field above. Missing values are not assumed normal. [8,9]

ABG and serial lactate

PaO₂ and FiO₂ calculate the respiratory SOFA component. Acid–base variables and lactate are clinical adjuncts and do not add SOFA points. Use a contemporaneous arterial sample for PaO₂; lactate can also be measured from venous blood. [9,10]

Enter ABG or lactate values for interpretation.

Sepsis-3 septic shock criteria require sepsis + vasopressor requirement to maintain MAP ≥65 mmHg + lactate >2 mmol/L despite adequate volume resuscitation. The two confirmation fields above are independent clinical assessments, not automatically inferred from SOFA drugs or MAP. Lactate is nonspecific; interpret changes alongside clinical perfusion and do not give additional fluid solely to normalize it. [9,10]

SOFA score: awaiting measurements
Enter the six organ system measurements to assess acute organ dysfunction.

Sepsis-3 screening requires suspected/confirmed infection plus an acute SOFA increase ≥2 points attributable to infection. A current SOFA ≥2 alone is insufficient without baseline assessment. SOFA does not diagnose septic shock; shock also requires clinical assessment including vasopressor and lactate criteria. The legacy SOFA calculation is distinct from SOFA-2.

SOFA inputs are adjuncts. A positive screening calculation can populate the clinical severity field; review and confirm before generating a regimen.

Supportive treatment instructions

Parallel tasks (first hour to first six hours bundle)

  1. Send cultures before first dose if feasible: blood ×2, sputum/ETA/NDBAL, urine, pus/drain.
  2. Source control within 6–12 h (drain/debride, remove/exchange infected lines & tubes).
  3. Hemodynamics: PLR and POCUS-guided balanced crystalloids (RL); noradrenaline infusion to MAP ≥65 mmHg; hydrocortisone 4 mg/kg IV bolus and 4 mg/kg IVI over 24 h for 3 days.
  4. Oxygenation target: PaO2 70 - 90 mmHg, SpO₂ 92–96%; avoid hyperoxia (avoid PaO2 > 120 mmHg for prolonged periods).
  5. Intubate and Ventilate if, GCS≤8 or inability to protect airway, or, Refractory Hypoxemia (PaO2/FiO2 <200 or SpO2 <90% on FiO2 >50% on >30 LPM flow on HFNC), or, Hypercapnic Respiratory Failure (PaCO2 ≥50 mmHg with pH <7.30), or, Signs of Impending Exhaustion (RR>33 bpm, tracheal tug, sweating, agitation, paradoxical breathing).
  6. Monitoring: mental status, capillary refill time, ABG, lactate trend, MAP, urine output, tympanic and peripheral temperature, 6am blood glucose, serum NT-proBNP, serum creatinine, INR; daily de-escalation review (24–48–72 h).
  7. Glycemia 140–180 mg/dL.
  8. Stress-ulcer prophylaxis - pantoprazole 40 mg IV/PO once daily.
  9. VTE prophylaxis, Enoxaparin 20 - 40 mg SC once daily (withhold if platelets < 80,000/micro L, or INR > 1.4).
  10. Early enteral nutrition, calorie dense diet / NGT formula feed (2 - 3 scoops/100mL), plus MCT oil 20 mL twice daily.
  11. Severe anemia correction, target Hb 8 - 10 g/dL; followup action, syrup multivitamin 15 mL once daily.
  12. Communicate with the family - present physical status, prognosis, ongoing treatment, short term treatment plan.
